Programming

When I was in 5th grade, I have this curiosity of how Minecraft Mods are made, like how can there be custom blocks, items, dimensions, etc. And as it turns out it's made by a list of words written in english, but not in a natural form, that somehow works and made sense to the game. When looking up a tutorial, I then know it involves using a programming language called Java. At the time I have no clue what I was getting myself into, so most of the time I would just copy everything that is done by the tutorial video I watched, and I remember being so proud of what I have made even though it was just a dummy block that has no texture on it. But little did I know back then that that's when I start gaining interest in typing code to create something from my imagination.

However, I stopped doing it ever since. It was because of how complex it is to continue turning my imaginations into reality that I finally burned out. Until the outbreak, where everyone stayed at home. The meta then was discord bots, which i got interested in as well. At this point I already developed a good sense of reading code, so I knew what I was typing and was going to make, and the language used this time was Python, which is perfect for high-level readibility, to me of course.

Soon enough, programming became the thing I have to learn more about and get into because of the school work that involve logical programming, not only it requires a high workload on my device, but it's also on my brain. And I hope it can be the primary thing I can eventually have a job on.

And that experience is what led me to being able to develop the sense of how can I edit a video to make it look spectacular outside of AMVs.
